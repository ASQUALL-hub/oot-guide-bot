# How Ocarina of Time fits together

What every item does, what each task unlocks, and the order the original game is played in. Generated by
`tools/build_game.py`; bots should read `game.json` (same data, plus every location name).

- **Curated** text is hand-written. Numbers (ammo, magic, age limits, damage, hookshot reach) come from the game code.
- **Spheres** come from replaying the original item layout through the randomizer's standard logic: sphere 0 is
  what you can get at the start, sphere N+1 is what sphere N's items open.
- **Strictly needed** means standard (glitchless) logic has no other way: the game was replayed from the start
  without that item or event, and these things stayed out of logic. Speedrun tricks and glitches may still get
  there without it, so this is never a claim that something is unreachable. **Directly needs** drops anything
  that another item on the list already needs (Light Arrows imply the Master Sword, and so on).

## Main quest

### 1. Get a sword and shield

Kokiri Sword: crawl through the hole in Kokiri Forest's training area to the chest. Deku Shield: buy it in the Kokiri Shop (40 rupees). Equip both, then Mido lets you pass to the Great Deku Tree.

- `KF Kokiri Sword Chest` → Kokiri Sword (sphere 0). Directly needs: nothing beyond reaching it (0 things in all).
- `event: Showed Mido Sword & Shield` (sphere 1). Directly needs: Deku Shield, Kokiri Sword (2 things in all).

### 2. Inside the Deku Tree

Slingshot is in the Deku Tree. Burn the web over the basement with a lit Deku Stick, defeat Queen Gohma (stun her eye with the slingshot or a Deku Nut, then slash). Reward: Kokiri's Emerald.

- `Deku Tree Slingshot Chest` → Slingshot (sphere 1). Directly needs: event: Showed Mido Sword & Shield (3 things in all).
- `Queen Gohma` → Kokiri Emerald (sphere 2). Directly needs: event: Defeat Queen Gohma (5 things in all).

### 3. Leave the forest

Saria gives the Fairy Ocarina on the bridge out of Kokiri Forest.

- `LW Gift from Saria` → Ocarina (sphere 2). Directly needs: event: Defeat Queen Gohma (5 things in all).

### 4. Hyrule Castle and Zelda

Malon gives the Weird Egg; it hatches into a Cucco that wakes Talon. Climb into the castle grounds, sneak to Zelda: Zelda's Letter. Impa teaches Zelda's Lullaby.

- `HC Malon Egg` → Weird Egg (sphere 2). Directly needs: event: Defeat Queen Gohma (5 things in all).
- `HC Zeldas Letter` → Zeldas Letter (sphere 3). Directly needs: Weird Egg (6 things in all).
- `Song from Impa` → Zeldas Lullaby (sphere 3). Directly needs: Weird Egg (6 things in all).

### 5. Death Mountain and the Gorons

Show Zelda's Letter at the Kakariko gate. Learn Saria's Song in the Sacred Forest Meadow and play it to Darunia: Goron's Bracelet.

- `event: Kakariko Village Gate Open` (sphere 4). Directly needs: Zeldas Letter (7 things in all).
- `Song from Saria` → Sarias Song (sphere 3). Directly needs: Weird Egg (6 things in all).
- `GC Darunias Joy` → Progressive Strength Upgrade (sphere 4). Directly needs: Ocarina, Sarias Song, Zeldas Lullaby, event: Kakariko Village Gate Open (11 things in all).

### 6. Dodongo's Cavern

Use Bomb Flowers (bracelet) to open the cave; the Bomb Bag is inside. King Dodongo: throw bombs into his mouth, then slash. Reward: Goron's Ruby.

- `Dodongos Cavern Bomb Bag Chest` → Bomb Bag (sphere 5). Directly needs: Progressive Strength Upgrade (12 things in all).
- `King Dodongo` → Goron Ruby (sphere 6). Directly needs: event: Defeat King Dodongo (14 things in all).

### 7. Zora's Domain and Jabu-Jabu

Play Zelda's Lullaby at the waterfall in Zora's River. Ruto's Letter (bottle) is under Lake Hylia: show it to King Zora, catch a fish, release it before Jabu-Jabu. The Boomerang is inside; Barinade needs it. Reward: Zora's Sapphire.

- `LH Underwater Item` → Rutos Letter (sphere 7). Directly needs: Progressive Scale (14 things in all).
- `Deliver Rutos Letter` → Deliver Letter (sphere 8). Directly needs: Rutos Letter (15 things in all).
- `Barinade` → Zora Sapphire (sphere 9). Directly needs: event: Defeat Barinade (19 things in all).

### 8. Door of Time

Zelda throws the Ocarina of Time into the castle moat (you learn the Song of Time). In the Temple of Time, play it with the three stones to open the Door, pull the Master Sword: Link is adult. Rauru gives the Light Medallion.

- `HF Ocarina of Time Item` → Ocarina (sphere 10). Directly needs: Goron Ruby, Kokiri Emerald, Zora Sapphire (23 things in all).
- `Song from Ocarina of Time` → Song of Time (sphere 10). Directly needs: Goron Ruby, Kokiri Emerald, Zora Sapphire (23 things in all).
- `Master Sword Pedestal` → Time Travel (sphere 11). Directly needs: Ocarina (the last of 2), Song of Time (25 things in all).
- `ToT Reward from Rauru` → Light Medallion (sphere 11). Directly needs: Ocarina (the last of 2), Song of Time (25 things in all).

### 9. Forest Temple

Hookshot: win Dampe's race inside his grave (as adult, pull the gravestone back to open it). Minuet of Forest from Sheik. Bow inside the temple. Phantom Ganon: shoot him with arrows, then return his energy balls. Reward: Forest Medallion.

- `Graveyard Dampe Race Hookshot Chest` → Progressive Hookshot (sphere 11). Directly needs: Time Travel (26 things in all).
- `Sheik in Forest` → Minuet of Forest (sphere 11). Directly needs: Time Travel (26 things in all).
- `Forest Temple Bow Chest` → Bow (sphere 13). Directly needs: Small Key (Forest Temple) (28 things in all).
- `Phantom Ganon` → Forest Medallion (sphere 15). Directly needs: event: Defeat Phantom Ganon (33 things in all).

### 10. Fire Temple

Goron Tunic from Link the Goron in Goron City (or buy one). Bolero of Fire. Megaton Hammer inside. Volvagia: hammer its head when it pops out. Reward: Fire Medallion.

- `GC Rolling Goron as Adult` → Goron Tunic (sphere 11). Directly needs: event: Stop GC Rolling Goron as Adult (27 things in all).
- `Sheik in Crater` → Bolero of Fire (sphere 12). Directly needs: Progressive Hookshot (27 things in all).
- `Fire Temple Megaton Hammer Chest` → Megaton Hammer (sphere 16). Directly needs: Small Key (Fire Temple) (29 things in all).
- `Volvagia` → Fire Medallion (sphere 18). Directly needs: event: Defeat Volvagia (32 things in all).

### 11. Ice Cavern and Water Temple

Thaw King Zora with Blue Fire for the Zora Tunic. Iron Boots in the Ice Cavern. In the Water Temple play Zelda's Lullaby on Triforce marks to change the water level; the Longshot is past Dark Link. Morpha: hookshot its nucleus out. Reward: Water Medallion.

- `ZD King Zora Thawed` → Zora Tunic (sphere 11). Directly needs: event: King Zora Thawed (27 things in all).
- `Ice Cavern Iron Boots Chest` → Iron Boots (sphere 12). Directly needs: Silver Rupee (Ice Cavern Push Block) (27 things in all).
- `Sheik in Ice Cavern` → Serenade of Water (sphere 12). Directly needs: Silver Rupee (Ice Cavern Push Block) (27 things in all).
- `Water Temple Longshot Chest` → Progressive Hookshot (sphere 21). Directly needs: Bow, Small Key (Water Temple), event: Raise Water Level (34 things in all).
- `Morpha` → Water Medallion (sphere 23). Directly needs: event: Defeat Morpha (37 things in all).

### 12. Bottom of the Well and Shadow Temple

Nocturne of Shadow in Kakariko. As child, play the Song of Storms in the windmill to drain the well: the Lens of Truth is at its bottom. Hover Boots in the Shadow Temple. Bongo Bongo: stun its hands, then its eye. Reward: Shadow Medallion.

- `Sheik in Kakariko` → Nocturne of Shadow (sphere 24). Directly needs: Fire Medallion, Forest Medallion, Water Medallion (49 things in all).
- `event: Drain Well` (sphere 12). Directly needs: Song of Storms (27 things in all).
- `Bottom of the Well Lens of Truth Chest` → Lens of Truth (sphere 12). Directly needs: event: Drain Well (28 things in all).
- `Shadow Temple Hover Boots Chest` → Hover Boots (sphere 25). Directly needs: Dins Fire, Lens of Truth, Magic Meter, Nocturne of Shadow (55 things in all).
- `Bongo Bongo` → Shadow Medallion (sphere 34). Directly needs: event: Defeat Bongo Bongo (62 things in all).

### 13. Gerudo Fortress and Spirit Temple

Free the four carpenters for the Gerudo card, cross the Haunted Wasteland to the Desert Colossus: Requiem of Spirit. Do the child half (Silver Gauntlets) and the adult half (Mirror Shield) of the Spirit Temple. Twinrova: reflect one sister's magic at the other with the Mirror Shield. Reward: Spirit Medallion.

- `Hideout Gerudo Membership Card` → Gerudo Membership Card (sphere 13). Directly needs: event: Hideout 1 Torch Jail Carpenter, event: Hideout 2 Torches Jail Carpenter, event: Hideout 3 Torches Jail Carpenter, event: Hideout 4 Torches Jail Carpenter (36 things in all).
- `Sheik at Colossus` → Requiem of Spirit (sphere 22). Directly needs: Lens of Truth, Magic Meter, Progressive Hookshot (the last of 2), event: GF Gate Open (50 things in all).
- `Spirit Temple Silver Gauntlets Chest` → Progressive Strength Upgrade (sphere 36). Directly needs: Small Key (Spirit Temple) (52 things in all).
- `Spirit Temple Mirror Shield Chest` → Mirror Shield (sphere 38). Directly needs: Small Key (Spirit Temple) (52 things in all).
- `Twinrova` → Spirit Medallion (sphere 40). Directly needs: event: Defeat Twinrova (55 things in all).

### 14. Light Arrows

With the Shadow and Spirit Medallions, return to the Temple of Time: Zelda gives the Light Arrows.

- `ToT Light Arrows Cutscene` → Light Arrows (sphere 41). Directly needs: Shadow Medallion, Spirit Medallion (80 things in all).

### 15. Ganon's Castle

The six medallions make the Rainbow Bridge. Clear the six trials (Light Arrows on each barrier), climb the tower, beat Ganondorf (Light Arrows, then sword), then Ganon (Light Arrows to stun, Master Sword on the tail).

- `event: Ganons Tower Access` (sphere 46). Directly needs: event: Fire Trial Clear, event: Forest Trial Clear, event: Light Trial Clear, event: Shadow Trial Clear, event: Spirit Trial Clear, event: Water Trial Clear (93 things in all).
- `Ganons Tower Boss Key Chest` → Boss Key (Ganons Castle) (sphere 46). Directly needs: event: Fire Trial Clear, event: Forest Trial Clear, event: Light Trial Clear, event: Shadow Trial Clear, event: Spirit Trial Clear, event: Water Trial Clear (93 things in all).
- `Ganon` → Triforce (sphere 47). Directly needs: Boss Key (Ganons Castle) (94 things in all).

## Pause menu: equipping things

- **open:** Press START. It is refused during scene transitions, cutscenes (Play_InCsMode), game over, while the magic bar is filling, and during the shooting gallery and bombchu bowling games (KaleidoSetup_Update in z_kaleido_setup.c).
- **pages:** Four pages in a ring: Select Item -> (R) Map -> (R) Quest Status -> (R) Equipment -> (R) Select Item. Z goes the other way. Within a scene the menu reopens on the last page used; loading a new scene resets it to Select Item (KaleidoSetup_Init sets pageIndex = PAUSE_ITEM).
- **item page:** A 6 x 4 grid. Slot number = row * 6 + column (row 0 at the top, column 0 at the left); `slot` in game.json items gives each item's slot. Move the cursor with the stick, then press C-left, C-down or C-right to put the item on that C button. Items the current age can't use are greyed out and can't be assigned.
- **equipment page:** A 4 x 4 grid. Rows: 0 swords, 1 shields, 2 tunics, 3 boots. Column 0 shows upgrades (bullet bag/quiver, bomb bag, strength, scale) and can't be selected for equipping; columns 1-3 are the three items of that row in the order Kokiri/Master/Biggoron, Deku/Hylian/Mirror, Kokiri/Goron/Zora, Kokiri/Iron/Hover. Press A to equip.
- **b button:** The B button holds the sword. Equipping a sword puts it on B. The Kokiri Sword is NOT put on B when you pick it up; the Master Sword is (code: Item_Give in z_parameter.c).
- **close:** Press START again.

## Combat

A hit's damage type is one bit (damage_bits). If the target has a damage table, the table row for that bit gives damage (low 4 bits) and a reaction code (high 4 bits); reaction codes are the actor's own (stun, fire, freeze...). Without a table the attack's own damage minus the target's defense is used, and the actor's code decides what happens (most bosses work this way). can_be_hit_by lists what each collider accepts at all.
